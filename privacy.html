Privacy & data deletion

Updated: 27 October 2026

This notice covers RushGlow Beauty & Skin Care's website and customer messaging services.

Information used for your order

When you contact us or place an order, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, phone number, delivery address, city, messages, selected products and payment details or payment proof. Please do not send passwords, one-time login codes, bank account passwords or card PINs.

How information is used

We use this information to answer questions, confirm and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, check payment, provide order updates and handle support requests. Automated replies may help with these tasks; you can ask for a person when you need help.

Who can access it

RushGlow's owner and authorised customer support or order staff can access relevant customer conversations and orders. Delivery providers receive information needed to deliver an order. Hosting, database, messaging and notification providers process information needed to operate these services. WhatsApp services are also subject to WhatsApp and Meta's own privacy terms.

Data deletion requests

To request access, correction or deletion of your customer information, email rushglow35@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Include the phone number used to contact us and, if available, your order number so we can locate the record and verify the request. Do not include passwords or OTPs. We will review your request and explain if information must be retained for an outstanding order, dispute or legal obligation.
