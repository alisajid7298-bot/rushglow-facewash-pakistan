Help / FAQ

Quick answers about shopping with RushGlow.

How much does delivery cost?
Delivery is free within Swabi District. Delivery outside Swabi District costs Rs. 200. Enter your city or district at checkout to see the delivery charge and total before placing your order.
Which payment methods can I use?
You can select EasyPaisa or Cash on Delivery at checkout. For Cash on Delivery, pay when your order arrives. For EasyPaisa, follow the payment instructions shown at checkout and upload your payment screenshot before placing the order.
How do I place an order?
Tap Buy Now on a product, or add products to your cart and tap Checkout. Enter your name, phone number, complete address and city, choose a payment method, then tap Place Order. Save the order number shown after your order is confirmed.
How can I track my order?
Open Track Your Order, enter the order number you received after checkout, and tap Track Order to see the latest status. If you cannot find your order number, contact us on WhatsApp.
When will my order arrive?
Delivery time depends on your location and the courier. Contact our team on WhatsApp for an estimated delivery time for your area. You can also check your order status in Track Your Order.
What if I have a payment or order problem?
If checkout shows an error or you are unsure whether your order was placed, check Track Your Order or contact our team before placing it again. For help with a payment, missing item, damaged product or return request, send your order number and details to us on WhatsApp.
Who can leave a product review?
Customers whose order is marked Delivery Complete can review a product they purchased. Tap Write a Review and enter your order number and the phone number used for the order. Reviews appear publicly after approval.
